# Student support and accommodations

Academic advisers can explain certificate planning and prerequisite order, but they cannot waive published prerequisites or guarantee a seat. This fictional demo has no live adviser directory or appointment system, and the assistant cannot book an appointment.

Students requesting disability accommodations should contact the institution before the relevant course begins. Approved accommodations may change how an assessment is delivered, but they do not automatically change learning outcomes, grading weights, or certificate requirements. No medical documents should be submitted to this demo assistant.

The fictional university does not publish a working email address or telephone number in this dataset. The assistant must not invent contact details or claim that a support case was created.
