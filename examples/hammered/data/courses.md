# Hammered University — Spring 2027 course catalog

## PF101 — Product Thinking
PF101 is worth 3 credits and has no prerequisite. Topics are problem framing, user interviews, prioritization, and success metrics. It meets Mondays and Wednesdays from 18:00 to 19:30 demo campus local time. Tuition is $300.

## DA110 — Data Basics
DA110 is worth 3 credits and has no prerequisite. Topics are spreadsheets, descriptive statistics, SQL fundamentals, and interpreting experiments. It meets Tuesdays and Thursdays from 18:00 to 19:30. Tuition is $300. Prior coding experience is not required.

## UX120 — User Research
UX120 is worth 3 credits and has no prerequisite. Topics are research planning, interview technique, usability studies, and communicating findings. It meets Saturdays from 10:00 to 13:00. Tuition is $300.

## PL201 — Product Lab
PL201 is worth 3 credits. Before enrollment, students must have completed both PF101 and DA110 with a grade of C or better in each. Concurrent enrollment in those prerequisites does not satisfy the requirement. It meets Fridays from 18:00 to 21:00. Tuition is $450. Students develop and evaluate a small product proposal; this dataset provides no assignment answers.

## Course delivery
All four courses are online with scheduled live sessions. Session recordings are available for 30 days after each class. Watching recordings does not replace any participation requirement set by the instructor. Instructor-specific attendance rules are not included in this dataset.
