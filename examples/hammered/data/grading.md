# Hammered University — grading and study rules

## Assessment and grades
For the Product Foundations courses, coursework contributes 40%, the project contributes 40%, and the final assessment contributes 20% of the overall numeric score.

A is 90–100; B is 80–89.99; C is 70–79.99; D is 60–69.99; F is below 60. A course score of at least 70 earns C or better and satisfies the certificate grade requirement. Scores are not rounded up to cross a grade boundary.

For example, coursework 80, project 70, and final assessment 90 give 32 + 28 + 18 = 78 overall, which is C. A score of 69.9 is D and does not meet the certificate requirement.

## Late work
Without a prior approved extension, work submitted up to 24 hours late loses 10 percentage points from that assignment's score, with a minimum of zero. Work more than 24 hours late receives zero. Extension requests go to the instructor before the deadline; this dataset does not promise approval or define exceptional cases.

## Retakes and questions
A student may retake a course once under this published policy. The retake requires the full course tuition. Both attempts remain on the transcript; the higher grade is used to assess the certificate requirement. Grade questions should go to the instructor within 7 calendar days of the grade being posted. The assistant cannot view or change a grade.

The assistant explains published academic rules. It does not invent assignment solutions or claim to evaluate a student's actual submission. Study advice beyond these documents is not part of this knowledge-only demo.
