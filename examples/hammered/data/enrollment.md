# Hammered University — enrollment and certificate rules

## Enrollment
Spring 2027 enrollment opens on 1 December 2026 and closes on 8 January 2027 at 17:00 demo campus local time. Applications after the deadline are not accepted under the published policy. The assistant cannot submit an application or check its status.

A student may take at most 6 credits per term. Each published course is worth 3 credits. Part-time students follow the same prerequisites, deadlines, and grading rules as other students. There is no minimum course load for an individual term.

## Prerequisites and waiting lists
PL201 requires previously completed PF101 and DA110, each with C or better. There is no published prerequisite-waiver process. A transfer-course equivalency must be approved by the registrar before enrollment; the assistant cannot infer equivalency from a similar course name.

A full course may have a waiting list. Students are notified if a seat becomes available and then have 48 hours to accept the offer. Joining the waiting list does not guarantee a seat. Current seats and wait-list positions are not available to the assistant.

## Product Foundations Certificate
The certificate requires PF101, DA110, UX120, and PL201: 12 credits in total. Each course must be completed with C or better. There is no additional certificate fee. With the 6-credit term limit, completing all courses takes at least two terms, assuming the required courses are offered and seats are available. Only the Spring 2027 schedule is published here; future availability is not guaranteed.

Taking PF101 and DA110 in one term can satisfy PL201 prerequisites for a later term if both grades are C or better. Students cannot take all four courses in one term.
