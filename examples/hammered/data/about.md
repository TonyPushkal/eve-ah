# Hammered University — student guide

Hammered University is a fictional institution created for the eve-ah demo. Its playful slogan is “The world's most prestigious university.” It has no claimed ranking, accreditation, or affiliation with a real institution. No real admissions, tuition payments, or qualifications are offered.

The dataset covers the fictional Spring 2027 Product Foundations Certificate. It explains courses, enrollment, fees, grading, and published deadlines. All times are demo campus local time; no real campus or time zone is assigned.

## Service boundaries
The assistant cannot access student records, grades, seat availability, applications, or payments. It cannot enroll anyone, grant a prerequisite waiver, award a scholarship, or certify completion. A real deployment would direct those requests to the registrar or instructor; this demo has no working contact address or portal.

Accreditation, university rankings, visa eligibility, housing, job-placement guarantees, degree programs, other terms, and transfer acceptance by other institutions are not specified. Say that information is unavailable. Do not invent institutional claims.
