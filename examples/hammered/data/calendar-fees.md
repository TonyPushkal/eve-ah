# Hammered University — Spring 2027 calendar and fees

## Key dates
Enrollment closes on 8 January 2027 at 17:00 demo campus local time. Classes begin on 18 January 2027. The add/drop deadline is 22 January 2027 at 17:00. The withdrawal deadline is 26 February 2027 at 17:00. Teaching ends on 9 April 2027. Final assessments take place from 12 through 16 April 2027.

The add/drop window is for students enrolled by the enrollment deadline; it is not an extension for late applications. Adding a course remains subject to prerequisites, seat availability, and the 6-credit limit.

## Tuition
PF101, DA110, and UX120 each cost $300. PL201 costs $450. The four-course certificate's listed tuition totals $1,350, excluding possible retake tuition. There is no additional certificate fee. Tuition for an enrolled course must be paid by 15 January 2027 at 17:00. This dataset does not specify late-payment penalties, installment plans, or scholarships.

## Dropping and withdrawing
Dropping a course by the add/drop deadline gives a full tuition refund and no transcript entry. After that deadline, withdrawing by the withdrawal deadline gives a W on the transcript and no tuition refund. W carries no grade points and does not count toward completed certificate credits.

After the withdrawal deadline, ordinary withdrawal is unavailable. Exceptional circumstances require registrar review; no approval criteria or guaranteed exception are published. Refund processing time is not specified.
