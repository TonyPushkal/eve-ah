# Certificates and academic records

The Product Foundations Certificate is awarded after all required courses are completed with passing grades and all tuition balances are cleared. A failed required course must be retaken under the published retake limit before the certificate can be awarded.

A digital certificate becomes available within 10 business days after the final grade is posted and the completion audit finishes. The date is an estimate, not a guarantee. Printed certificates are not offered in this demo.

An unofficial grade summary is visible in the learning platform. The assistant cannot access a student's grades, completion audit, balance, certificate status, or transcript. Official transcript ordering and delivery fees are not specified in this dataset.
