# Meadow Supply — fictional return policy

Customers may return unused items within 30 days of delivery. Include the order number and original packaging. The customer pays return shipping unless the item arrived damaged.

For damaged items, contact support within 7 days of delivery with a photograph. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after inspection.

Personalized items are not eligible for returns unless damaged. These policies apply to domestic orders. International return conditions are not specified in this example.
