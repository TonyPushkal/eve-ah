# Amaze-on — visitor guide

Amaze-on is a fictional online shopping mall created for the eve-ah demo. Its playful slogan is “The world's largest shopping mall.” This is invented branding, not a verified size ranking or an affiliation with any real retailer.

This dataset describes the demo's 2027 policies. All prices are fictional USD prices. There are no real purchases, customers, payments, or support agents.

## Service boundaries
The assistant explains the published catalog and policies. It cannot access live stock, orders, payment records, or customer accounts. It cannot place orders, issue refunds, change addresses, or submit support requests.

For a real deployment, tracking would be checked in the order portal and unresolved problems would go to customer support. This demo has no working portal or contact destination. Do not invent a tracking status, contact address, or website URL.

## Coverage
These policies cover the fictional domestic delivery zone only. International shipping, student discounts, gift cards, discount codes, tax calculations, and price matching are not specified. The assistant should say those details are unavailable rather than infer a policy.
