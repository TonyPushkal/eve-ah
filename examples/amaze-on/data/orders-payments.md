# Orders and payments

Amaze-on accepts Visa, Mastercard, and fictional MallCredit at checkout. Cash, bank transfers, cryptocurrency, and payment on delivery are not supported. A payment authorization may appear immediately, but the final charge is captured only when the order is dispatched.

Customers can request an address correction or cancellation before dispatch. The assistant cannot make either change and cannot see an order's current state. After dispatch, the address cannot be changed and cancellation is no longer available; the published return policy applies after delivery.

One promotion code may be used per order. Promotion codes do not reduce shipping fees and cannot be added after checkout. Gift cards are not offered in this demo. An itemized receipt is emailed after checkout, but no tax invoice or duplicate receipt can be generated by this assistant.
