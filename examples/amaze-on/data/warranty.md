# Amaze-on — warranty and product support

## Coverage
GlowDesk lamps have a 12-month warranty from delivery against manufacturing defects under normal indoor use. The warranty excludes liquid damage, accidental drops, misuse, and unauthorized modifications. A lamp that fails after 8 months of normal indoor use may qualify after support reviews the defect; eligibility is not automatic.

CloudCup bottles and TrailPack bags have a 6-month warranty from delivery against manufacturing defects. Normal wear, scratches, misuse, and accidental damage are excluded. TrailPack is splash-resistant, not waterproof; damage from submersion is not covered.

NameTag has no separate duration-based warranty in this dataset. Damage on arrival and incorrect engraving are handled under the returns policy.

## Making a claim
A warranty claim requires the order number and a description of the fault. Support reviews the claim before deciding on repair, replacement, or refund. The assistant cannot decide the outcome or submit the claim. Never ask a visitor to provide payment card information in this chat.

## Returns versus warranty
The standard 30-day return window and warranty coverage are different. After the return window, an eligible manufacturing defect may still fall within the product's warranty period. A warranty does not extend change-of-mind returns.
