# Product care and troubleshooting

If GlowDesk does not turn on, confirm that the included USB-C cable is firmly connected to a powered wall adapter. The wall adapter is not included. Disconnect power for 30 seconds before reconnecting it. Do not open the lamp housing; opening it voids warranty coverage. If these steps fail, the assistant can explain the warranty but cannot create a claim.

CloudCup must be hand-washed with mild detergent and left open to dry. It is not safe for a dishwasher, microwave, freezer, or stovetop. Odours are not a manufacturing defect unless support review identifies a material fault.

TrailPack should be spot-cleaned with a damp cloth and air-dried. Machine washing, tumble drying, bleach, and submersion may damage its water-resistant coating. NameTag should be wiped with a dry cloth; engraving cannot be altered after production begins.
