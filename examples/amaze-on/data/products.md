# Amaze-on — product catalog

These are fictional list prices, not live offers. Current stock and tax are not available.

## CloudCup insulated bottle
CloudCup costs $24 and holds 750 ml. Its stainless-steel body keeps drinks cold for up to 12 hours or hot for up to 6 hours under the fictional product specification. Hand-wash it; do not put it in a dishwasher or microwave. It is not an electronic product.

## GlowDesk task lamp
GlowDesk costs $48. It has three brightness levels and a USB-C power cable; a wall adapter is not included. It has no battery and must remain connected to power. Use it indoors in dry conditions. Disconnect power before wiping it with a dry cloth.

## TrailPack day bag
TrailPack costs $65 and has a 20-litre capacity and a padded compartment for laptops up to 14 inches. Its fabric resists light splashes but is not waterproof. Do not submerge it or machine-wash it. Wipe clean and air-dry.

## NameTag personalized luggage tag
NameTag costs $12. The buyer can supply up to 12 letters, including spaces, for engraving. Production takes 3 business days before dispatch. Because it is personalized, the standard change-of-mind return policy does not apply; damaged or incorrectly engraved tags are handled under the returns policy.
