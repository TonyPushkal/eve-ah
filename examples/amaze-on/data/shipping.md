# Amaze-on — shipping and delivery

## Shipping rates
Standard domestic shipping costs $5 for a merchandise subtotal below $50. Standard shipping is free at $50 or more. The threshold uses the subtotal after discounts and before tax or shipping. Express shipping costs $12 at every subtotal; the free-standard-shipping offer does not waive express fees.

## Timing
In-stock, non-personalized orders placed before 14:00 demo local time on a business day dispatch within 1 business day. Orders placed at or after 14:00 enter processing the next business day. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding published holidays; this dataset contains no holiday calendar.

Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days after dispatch. Express delivery takes 1–2 business days after dispatch. These are estimates, not guarantees. Production time is additional for personalized products: NameTag requires 3 business days before dispatch. Express changes transit speed, not production time.

## Delivery exceptions
If an order has not arrived 2 business days after the latest estimated delivery day, ask support to investigate. This demo cannot check tracking or submit the request. Exact arrival dates require dispatch information and the holiday calendar, which the assistant does not have.
