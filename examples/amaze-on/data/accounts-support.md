# Accounts and support

Customers may check out as a guest or create an account. An account stores saved addresses and order history, but it does not change product prices, shipping fees, return windows, or warranty coverage. The assistant cannot sign in to an account, reset a password, read order history, or change account details.

This fictional demo has no working email address, telephone number, or human-support queue. In a real deployment, the operator would configure its own contact destination. Do not invent a contact address or claim that a support request was opened.

The storefront supports keyboard navigation and current versions of major browsers. Accessibility feedback is welcome in a real deployment, but no live feedback channel exists in this fictional dataset.
