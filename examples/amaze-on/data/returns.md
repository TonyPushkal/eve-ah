# Amaze-on — returns and refunds

## Standard returns
Unused, non-personalized products may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. Include the order number, original packaging, and all included accessories. A customer opening the box does not by itself make the product ineligible, provided the product remains unused and the packaging and accessories are retained.

The customer pays return postage for a change-of-mind return. Original shipping charges are not refunded for change-of-mind returns. The assistant cannot approve or process a return.

## Damaged or wrong products
Report a product that arrived damaged or a wrong product within 7 calendar days of delivery. Support would request the order number and a photo through an appropriate support channel. Amaze-on pays return postage for a confirmed damaged or wrong item, including a replacement that itself arrives damaged. The same 7-day report window applies to a damaged replacement, measured from its delivery.

Personalized NameTags cannot be returned for a change of mind. A damaged tag or engraving that differs from the submitted text qualifies for a replacement or refund after review. A spelling error in text correctly supplied by the buyer does not qualify under the engraving-error policy.

## Refund timing
Approved refunds go to the original payment method within 5 business days after inspection. The inspection date is not the delivery date. No inspection turnaround or bank processing estimate is provided. Do not promise an exact refund date.
